# Vela Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Vela For Tesla (Vela) is developed by Baodi Shan. This policy describes how the app handles information.

## Information handled on your device

Vela connects directly to your Tesla over Bluetooth. It uses vehicle information, including speed, gear, battery and charging status, climate settings, tire pressure, media information, and vehicle location, to display the dashboard and perform controls you request. Vela does not send this information to a server operated by the developer.

The vehicle identification number (VIN) and app preferences are stored locally on your iPhone. The cryptographic private key used to communicate with your vehicle is stored in the iOS Keychain. Vela does not require a Vela account or your Tesla account password.

## Maps and vehicle services

When online, Vela uses Apple's MapKit to display a map around the vehicle's location. Apple may process map requests and related information under [Apple Maps & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/). Map display is not an entirely offline feature.

Nearby Supercharger information is requested from your vehicle over Bluetooth. The vehicle's own connected services are governed by Tesla's privacy practices.

## Analytics, advertising, and diagnostics

Vela contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. The app writes diagnostic messages to the device's system log for troubleshooting; it does not automatically upload those logs to the developer.

## Your choices and removal

You can disable Bluetooth access in iOS Settings. Using Vela's Remove vehicle control removes the locally saved VIN and the associated private key. To revoke the phone key's authorization at the vehicle, also remove that key using your Tesla's key settings. App preferences can be removed by deleting the app. Device backups and system-managed records are subject to your Apple settings.

## Support

For support or privacy questions, use [Vela's GitHub support page](https://github.com/lwshanbd/Vela/issues). Information you choose to submit there is processed by GitHub and is visible publicly. Do not post your VIN, vehicle keys, precise location, or other sensitive information. The developer uses information you submit to respond to your request.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The current version and its update date will be available on this page.
